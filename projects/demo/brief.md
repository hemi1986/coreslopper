# Brief — Neon Rain

## Topic

A short, neon-soaked synthwave piece about a night walk through a rain-lit city. The demo exists to prove the workflow end-to-end, not to be a finished commercial release.

## Source and commission

Original fixture project for the coreslopper repository. The music is a synthetic, license-safe demo track; the lyrics are original.

## Audience

People evaluating the workflow, plus anyone who wants a 12-second example of the motion-only pipeline. Real projects can target YouTube, Shorts, Reels, TikTok, or a private archive.

## Length

12 seconds for this demo. Real projects default to 2:30–4:00.

## Formats

- Master: 16:9, 1920×1080, 30 fps.
- Derivatives: 9:16 1080×1920 and 1:1 1080×1080.
- 4K/60 is out of scope for the demo.

## Style

Synthwave / cyberpunk: deep near-black backgrounds, hot pink and cyan neon, acid-yellow highlights, Orbitron display type, Space Grotesk body type, JetBrains Mono technical labels. Rain, chrome, grid horizons, scanlines, restrained glitch.

## Constraints

- Motion-only. No AI clips or footage in this demo.
- Budget: $50, but the demo should not spend anything.
- No secrets, no copyrighted media, no Suno/fal.ai/Higgsfield output committed.
- Everything must pass the schema and coverage validators.

## Success criteria

- The demo renders without manual asset production.
- Six shots cover 0–12 seconds with no gaps or overlaps.
- The contact sheet and review report are generated.
- A second render produces the same frame hashes.
