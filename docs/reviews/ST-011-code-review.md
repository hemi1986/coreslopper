Verdict: fix blockers first.
Most important: #1. The keep decision is per sound in `keptSounds` but per line in voice-track and episode-track, so a second line naming a removed sound crashes on `take!.text_hash` (reproduced), or passes off a stale take as the kept copy.

## Resolution (main session)

- #1 blocker – fixed. `keptSounds` keeps a removed sound only when every line naming it has its own current take (its `library:<id>` copy or the user's import); `expectedTakeSource` keeps a take only when it is the line's own `library:<line.sound>` copy. Regression test: "stops on a second line naming a removed sound that has no take of its own".
- #2 major – fixed. A copy's text hash comes from `takeSource(line, sound)`. Regression test: "copies a sound line whose imported take was deleted with the hash of the sound".
- #3 major – fixed. `workspace/.claude/skills/voice-track/SKILL.md` names memes, `library:<id>`, the copy, the warning and the new stop.
- Question (imported take, removed sound) – decided by recommendation, not reviewed by the user: a current imported take keeps the line, like a library copy (HS-260: a meme does not stop on a removed sound).
- #5 minor – fixed: the test also checks that no manifest entry was written.
- #6 minor – partly: the per-line decision is one rule in core (`expectedTakeSource`) plus one in `keptSounds`; both callers share `projectLibrarySounds`. A single module returning canon and per-line expectation is not done – the two rules now agree, and a deeper module is a refactor beyond the story.
- #7 minor – skipped: `scriptSounds` stays as the seam of the ST-009 tests.
- #8 minor – skipped: the copied id pattern and the ambience schema are low urgency; no code writes a library ambience.
- #9 minor – fixed: `-ss`/`-t` use `toFixed(6)`; a missing `sound.wav` is a ValidationError.
