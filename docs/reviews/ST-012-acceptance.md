Verdict: **accepted with remarks**

All four scenarios have a test with the exact title that sets up the scenario and asserts its literal outcome, and all pass. Round 1's gaps are closed: scenario 1 runs the real meme fixture through `validateProject` and checks 6.12 s and frame 184; the queue blocker is fixed and covered (`buildShotItems` loads the cues, and the scenario-1 test would throw without them); the removed-but-kept sound now fails with a truthful message; the cue offset is asserted at 6.37 s; `resolveStoryboard` takes one `StoryboardContext`; and the format rule is one shared helper. The remaining remarks are test/spec precision, none a scenario failure: the two refusal tests anchor the cue on the preceding shot's `end` rather than a shot's `start` (same resolver branch); scenario 3's asserted message says "has no sound cue" where the story says "has no cue" (glossary term, one-word drift); the new "no longer in the sound library" message has no test; and the `cue` edge's schema-wide acceptance and the story's 30-second wording remain low spec questions.
