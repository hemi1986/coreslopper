# Night Desk — series bible

A hand-written demo canon. It exists to exercise episode projects end to end on the mock provider; it is not a production series.

## Premise

Mara works the night shift of an IT help desk in an office tower that empties at ten. Nobody calls after midnight. One night an unknown contact starts writing to her through the internal chat, and every message seems to know a little more about the building than it should.

## Tone

Quiet, lonely, slightly uncanny. No jump scares: tension comes from silence, small sounds, and text appearing on a screen. Dialogue is sparse and spoken under the breath.

## World rules

- The story happens in one office tower at night. Each scene plays in one set of the canon.
- The unknown contact never appears on screen. It exists only as chat messages (`screen-only`).
- The building announcement is a voice without a face (`voice-only`).
- Spoken lines are German; every spoken and chat line has an English subtitle.

## Characters

See `characters.yaml`. Mara is the only character whose face is shown.

## Sets

See `sets.yaml`: the open-plan office at night and the elevator lobby.
