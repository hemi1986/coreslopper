# {{TITLE}} — series bible

The written canon of the series `{{SLUG}}`. The `new-series` skill fills it in with the user; every episode reads it and none changes it.

## Premise

What the series is about, in two or three sentences.

## Tone

How it feels: pace, humour, tension, what it never does.

## World rules

The rules every episode keeps: where it plays, what is possible, how recurring characters appear (display rules), and the languages of speech and subtitles.

## Characters

Who recurs, and what never changes about them. The machine-readable descriptions, references, and reference voices are in `characters.yaml`.

## Sets

The recurring locations. Their descriptions, prompt anchors, and reference frames are in `sets.yaml`.
