## Verdict

**accepted with remarks** — all three scenarios have an exact-title test that builds its Given from real files, goes through the real entry point (`runAutopilot` / `autopilotOverview`), and asserts the scenario's literal Then; scenarios 1 and 2 are discriminating (they fail without the guards), and scenario 3 is the public negative control. The targeted run, the related release tests, the full suite, typecheck, the spec linter, `check-scenarios` and `verify.ts` are green at `6cc6564`, and nothing outside the autopilot, its tests, the story status and the generated backlog changed. The story's rule that the release status, the autopilot log and the earlier files stay as they are holds in code (the guard returns before any write), though no scenario pins the status or a non-empty log; that, the mid-round distribution race and the stale `--plan` help text are the remarks above, not a scenario without a real test, a failing test or a violated rule.
