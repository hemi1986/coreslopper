# Treatment — Neon Rain

## Core idea

A figure we never fully see moves through a city that feels like a circuit board in the rain. The camera is close, the neon is loud, and the typography behaves like a signal surfacing from the noise.

## Visual world

- **Palette:** near-black `#050510`, hot pink `#ff2a6d`, cyan `#05d9e8`, acid yellow `#f9f002`, off-white text.
- **Texture:** wet asphalt, scanlines, chromatic fringes, thin grid lines, soft volumetric glow.
- **Type:** Orbitron for titles, Space Grotesk for lyric lines, JetBrains Mono for technical labels.
- **Motion:** slow pushes, beat-synced pulses, particles drifting upward, a single glitch hit at the end.

## Dramaturgy

1. **Intro (0–2 s):** The title “NEON RAIN” surfaces through the grid.
2. **Verse (2–5 s):** “City lights are calling” — the camera pushes through a rain-lit corridor.
3. **Chorus (5–9 s):** “We are electric” and “Run through the rain” — the palette opens up, particles accelerate, the type gets larger and brighter.
4. **Outro (9–12 s):** “Neon hearts” resolves to the coreslopper mark, then a short glitch cut to black.

## Shot mix

100 % motion graphics for the first vertical slice. A later version of this treatment can turn the verse and chorus into AI clips or footage while keeping the same timing anchors.
