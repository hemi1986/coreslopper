Verdict: **accepted with remarks**

All four scenarios have a test with the exact title that sets up the scenario's data and asserts the scenario's literal outcome, and all pass. The implementation matches the rules the scenarios touch: the cue resolves to the line start plus the library sound's cue, a non-sound line and a sound without a cue are refused with the scenario's wording, and the second format fails through the real `validateProject` seam. Typecheck, the full suite and `verify.ts` are green, the schema is the source for the new edge, and the glossary and the storyboard skill document the meme anchor.

The remarks are for the developer and the user, none of them a scenario failure: the cue map built in `validateProject` has no end-to-end test (the three resolver tests hand it in), the queue's `clipSeconds` fallback would throw on a cue-anchored clip when `motion.props.clipSeconds` is unset (a consequence of HS-79), a removed-but-kept sound loses its cue with a misleading message, the cue offset is untested, the `cue` edge is schema-wide while only memes can resolve it, and the format rule's early placement and the story's 30 s wording are spec/design questions.
