## Checked and fine

- The three scenarios have tests with the exact titles and each checks its "Then"; the folder has only `video/`, `cover/`, `texts/`, `calendar.ics`, `README.md` and `manifest.json` for a meme.
- Every music-video part is gated by kind in `buildRelease`: episode/music subtitles, thumbnails, Shorts, the audio/streaming block (WAV, canvas, lyrics), `deriveReleaseMedia` and the microsite. `calendarInput` suppresses announcement, community post, feed post, X, LinkedIn and microsite.
- `expectedTexts('meme')` matches `releaseTexts`: `youtube.md`, `tiktok.md`, `instagram.md`, `release-plan.md`. QA skips the audio/streaming/thumbnail/Shorts checks and checks the 1080x1920 cover.
- Credits are deduplicated by sound id and sorted; a sound without a credit adds none; the take source is schema-validated and `loadLibrarySound` validates the id. The credit is written into the texts, never logged; no personal data or shell input.
- `check-language.ts` passes; `npm run typecheck` is clean; the release, calendar, autopilot and characterization tests pass (72 tests).
- The board writes remain behind `trustedWrite`; this diff adds no new route or write.

Verdict: **fix blockers first**
Most important finding: **#1** – the meme's limit check runs on a caption without the credits the written texts carry, so a credit can push a TikTok or Instagram caption over its limit while `release` and QA both report success.
